# M3, experiment E1: is thinned foliage good enough at arm's length?

Written 2026-10-14. Spec section 5.6.4 ("Experiment E1"), risk 1, section 10 (budget re-base). Paths below are relative
to `build/` unless they start with `Assets/`.

## Decision

**Arm C: the untouched scan is the nearest level of every young fir, out to 6 m.** Arms A and B do not pass at
`fir_close` beside arm C; arm C's frame time at `crouch_hide` is inside the 11 ms budget, so the distance stays 6 m (the
spec shortens it to 4 m only when the budget is missed). `ForestKitSpec.ScanLodDistance` is 6 for
`fir_sapling_medium_a/b/c` and 0 for the tall firs, as the spec requires.

Behind the scan the thinned levels were then retuned so that the tree does not change species where they take over
(risk 1, fallback b). The final young fir has four levels: the scan to 6 m, then 52,000 to 61,000 flattened needle quads
to 14 m, then 8,000 to 42 m, then 1,200 to 85 m.

**For review 2 (go or no-go for M4), the number that matters:** with the existing planner's 771 young firs and no tall
fir, the frame by stopwatch is **10.92 ms median (p95 11.87) at `crouch_hide`, 10.22 at `fir_close`, 10.17 at
`trail_start`, against a budget of 11.0 ms and a warning line of 9.5 ms.** The headroom at the worst view is 0.08 ms.
The chosen young firs cost 1.2 ms more there than arm A did (9.71 ms at `crouch_hide`). The tall firs of M4 have no room left unless the
knobs of spec section 10 are turned first (knob 1, the per-camera decal frame setting that removes the forced depth
prepass, has not been tried). The stopwatch serialises CPU and GPU and is an upper bound (probe P5); `FrameTiming` still
reports only 7 or 8 of 240 frames.

- **What a piece is.** The thinner found 43,812 to 71,126 separate pieces per tree, median 8 triangles: each one is a
  single needle card (43,154 of the `b`) or a stem strip (658 of the `b`). No welded twig, so no spatial fallback. This
  is what makes enlargement fail: a kept piece grows as one needle.
- **Arm A fails.** At 3.2 times life size the needles are long flat blades; the thicket at `fir_close` and `crouch_hide`
  reads as bamboo or a palm-like shrub, not a fir.
- **Arm B fails.** At 1.7 times the needles are visibly coarser and sparser than the scan beside it; it reads as a
  coarser conifer with loose straps, and the twig's comb-like rows of fine needles are gone.
- **Arm C passes by definition.** It is the reference: fine needles in rows on thin twigs, a young fir at 1 to 3 m.

## Budget re-base (spec section 10)

"If E1 chooses a denser nearest level, the triangle, vertex and mesh-memory budgets are re-based on the measured
numbers of the chosen arm in the same change, and the frame-time budget stays where it is." The budgets are tunables
owned by `ForestBudgets` with a copy in `Tests/ForestPlayMode/ForestPlayBudgets.cs`; both were changed together (rule 5),
and `Budgets_PlayModeCopyMatches` compares them.

| Budget | Was (spec, for arm A) | Now | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Triangles (warning) | 8.0 M (6.5 M) | 11.6 M (10.1 M) | Plus what the chosen arm measured over arm A at `crouch_hide`, the worst view: 12,482,801 - 8,901,125 = 3.58 M |
| Vertices (warning) | 10.0 M (8.0 M) | 19.0 M (17.0 M) | Plus 17,019,198 - 8,058,338 = 8.96 M (a flattened needle is 4 vertices for 2 triangles) |
| Mesh memory (warning) | 300 MiB (220) | 400 MiB (320) | Plus the three scans: 1,747,107 vertices of 48 bytes and 1,533,513 triangles of 32-bit indices, 97.6 MiB (the spec estimated about 105) |
| Frame time | 11.0 ms | 11.0 ms | Unchanged: it is the gate |

**The triangle budget still fails in this scene**, and not because of E1: arm A itself measured 8.90 M at
`crouch_hide` against the spec's 8.0 M. The re-based budget carries the same overshoot (12.48 M against 11.6 M, 0.88 M
over, as arm A was 0.90 M over 8.0 M). It comes from the existing planner's 771 young firs (spec 7.2 estimated about 770)
on top of the 3.82 M floor (`STATE.md`, M2). `Budget_Counts` in the forest PlayMode suite fails on it. Bringing it under
is part of placing the trees (next stage) or of the section-10 knobs; the budget was not raised to hide it.
