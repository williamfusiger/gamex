## What the owner asked for (2026-10-15)

He noticed the trees have no wind, and that a dinosaur walking through foliage with no reaction would look wrong. He wants both handled now, before the island map and the dinosaur models. He accepts reducing the distance over which wind is animated if performance needs it.

What we agreed with him, as the shape of the solution:
1. Wind in the vertex shader: slow sway of trunks and large branches, faster motion at branch tips and fern fronds, gusts that vary over time and space, all from a global wind direction and strength. No physics.
2. Foliage that parts around bodies: the same shader pushes nearby vertices away from a small set of "pushers" (the player now, dinosaurs later), so ferns part and low fir branches bend when something passes through. No physics.
3. Trunks stay solid colliders. Only the thinnest young-fir stems may be passable (the coordinator's default, not yet confirmed by the owner); everything thicker blocks the player and the NavMesh as today.
4. Wind is animated only near the camera if needed for performance (the fog hides detail beyond about 30 m); far LODs may be static, with no visible pop where motion stops.
5. Rustle sounds and stealth noise from moving through vegetation come later, with the dinosaurs. Leave a clean hook for them (an event or query for "a pusher is moving through dense foliage here"), but no audio now.
